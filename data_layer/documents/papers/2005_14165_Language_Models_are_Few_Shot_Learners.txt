======================================================================
Abstract
======================================================================

Recent work has demonstrated substantial gains on many NLP tasks and benchmarks by pre-training on a large corpus of text followed by fine-tuning on a specific task. While typically task-agnostic in architecture, this method still requires task-specific fine-tuning datasets of thousands or tens of thousands of examples. By contrast, humans can generally perform a new language task from only a few examples or from simple instructions - something which current NLP systems still largely struggle to do. Here we show that scaling up language models greatly improves task-agnostic, few-shot performance, sometimes even reaching competitiveness with prior state-of-the-art fine-tuning approaches. Specifically, we train GPT-3, an autoregressive language model with 175 billion parameters, 10x more than any previous non-sparse language model, and test its performance in the few-shot setting. For all tasks, GPT-3 is applied without any gradient updates or fine-tuning, with tasks and few-shot demonstrations specified purely via text interaction with the model. GPT-3 achieves strong performance on many NLP datasets, including translation, question-answering, and cloze tasks, as well as several tasks that require on-the-fly reasoning or domain adaptation, such as unscrambling words, using a novel word in a sentence, or performing 3-digit arithmetic. At the same time, we also identify some datasets where GPT-3&#39;s few-shot learning still struggles, as well as some datasets where GPT-3 faces methodological issues related to training on large web corpora. Finally, we find that GPT-3 can generate samples of news articles which human evaluators have difficulty distinguishing from articles written by humans. We discuss broader societal impacts of this finding and of GPT-3 in general.

## 1. 背景动机

2018-2019 年 NLP 的主流范式是 pretrain + finetune:用大型文本语料预训练一个语言模型
(GPT-1/BERT/RoBERTa/T5 等),再在下游任务的小数据集上做 finetune。这种范式需要为每个任务
收集训练样本,在工程上成本很高。

作者团队(OpenAI)在 GPT-2(2019)上观察到,随着参数量和数据量增加,语言模型在 zero-shot
任务上出现了明显的趋势性提升——这暗示只要模型够大,也许根本不需要 finetune,
通过 in-context learning(在 prompt 里给几个示例)就能完成下游任务。

## 2. 核心方法

GPT-3 沿用 GPT-2 的 decoder-only Transformer 架构,改动不大:把层归一化放到每个
Transformer block 的输入侧(pre-norm),并把模型沿深度和宽度扩展到 175B 参数。
训练数据采用多种高质量语料混合(Common Crawl、Wikipedia、Books、WebText 等),
总 token 数约 300B,数据清洗和质量加权做了大量工作。

评测采用三种 setting:
  - Zero-shot:只在 prompt 里给任务描述;
  - One-shot:加 1 个示例;
  - Few-shot(in-context):在 context window 内塞 K 个示例(K 通常 10~100),
    不做梯度更新。

论文系统测试了 42 个任务:翻译、问答、完形填空、阅读理解、常识推理、算术、新闻生成、
代码补全、Adversarial NLI 等。

## 3. 关键结果

核心结论:模型规模从 125M → 175B,在几乎所有任务上,zero-shot/one-shot/few-shot 性能都单调上升,
而且 few-shot 相对 zero-shot 的差距也在扩大——说明大模型越来越能"读懂 prompt 里的示例"。

亮点:Closed-book QA(TriviaQA)上,zero-shot 已超越微调的 T5-11B;LAMBADA 常识完形填空上,
few-shot 76.2% 准确率,刷新 SOTA;算术、单词反转、新闻文章生成等任务,小模型几乎做不了,
大模型却出现明显能力跃迁。

局限:在一些"对比/逻辑推理"任务(如 NLI)上,虽然有提升但仍远低于 SOTA,说明纯 scale up
并不能解决所有问题。数据/算力外推到某个点后,这些能力仍需要专门设计。

## 4. 局限与后续影响

GPT-3 这篇 70 页论文宣告了"大模型时代"的正式到来。它用 42 个任务的系统实验给出了
强有力的 evidence,说服了整个社区:scale is all you need——把数据、参数、算力堆起来,
in-context learning 就能替代 finetune。这直接催生了后来的 Codex(代码)、DALL·E(图像)、
ChatGPT(RLHF + 对话)。

也带来一个新的研究范式:prompt engineering(因为不再 finetune,prompt 怎么写变得至关重要)、
in-context learning 理论(为什么 context 里的几个示例能让模型"学会"新任务)、
以及 emergent abilities(scaling 过程中突然出现的能力)等热门话题。

对工程界,175B 模型的训练/推理成本也推动了模型并行(张量并行、流水并行)、
FlashAttention、KV cache 等一系列系统和算法优化。
